Privacy Policy

Simple Currency Converter • Last updated: October 8, 2026

Simple Currency Converter ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed.

Data Collection

The app collects no data whatsoever, not even for improving the user experience:

  • No account, sign-in, or registration.
  • No personal data (name, email, location) is collected.
  • No analytics data is collected.
  • The app contains no ads, and no data is processed for advertising.

Internet Usage

To fetch current rates, the app sends a standard request to the public rates service (open.er-api.com) on each launch. The request contains no information identifying you. If the request fails, the app uses the last rates stored on your device.

On-Device Storage

The last downloaded rates are stored only on your own device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

User Rights

As no data is collected, there is no stored data subject to access, rectification, or erasure requests. You may still contact us with any privacy question.

Data Protection

We take the security of your data seriously. Rate data stored on your device is protected by iOS's standard app sandboxing and is never shared with or sold to third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be published on this page and takes effect immediately upon posting. You are advised to review this page periodically.
